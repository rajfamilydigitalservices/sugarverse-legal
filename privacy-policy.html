Sugarverse Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

Sugarverse ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Sugarverse mobile application and related services.

1. Age Requirement (18+)

Sugarverse is strictly intended for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If we become aware that a user is under 18, their account and data are terminated immediately.

2. Information We Collect

  • Profile Information: Name, age, gender, photos, bio, interests, and relationship preferences.
  • Device Permissions:
    • Camera & Photos: To allow you to take profile pictures and submit photos for identity verification.
    • Location: To show you potential matches in your geographical area and power Travel Mode.
  • Communications: Messages and interactions between matched users.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly to provide discovery, matching, secure real-time messaging, safety verification, and fraud prevention within the Sugarverse ecosystem.

4. Data Security & Storage

Your data is encrypted both in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We implement strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring that your private messages and personal settings are inaccessible to unauthorized parties.

5. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to access, edit, or permanently delete your account and all associated personal data at any time directly through Settings > Privacy & Security > Delete Account or by contacting support at support@sugarverse.app.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our Data Protection Team at privacy@sugarverse.app.
