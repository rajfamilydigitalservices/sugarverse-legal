Sugarverse Account & Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play Developer policies and international data protection laws (GDPR & CCPA), Sugarverse users have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data, as well as the deletion of specific data categories without closing their account.

1. How to Delete Your Entire Account & Associated Data

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant)

You can instantly and permanently delete your account directly inside the Sugarverse mobile application at any time:

  1. Open the Sugarverse app.
  2. Navigate to Profile > Settings (gear icon).
  3. Select Privacy & Security.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your choice. Your account, profile photos, matches, chats, and personal data are erased immediately.

Method 2: Web / Email Account Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or are unable to access your device, you can request full account deletion by emailing our Data Protection Officer:

Email: privacy@sugarverse.app

Please include the email address or phone number registered with your Sugarverse account. Requests are processed within 48 hours.

2. How to Delete Specific Data Without Deleting Your Account (Manage App Data)

If you wish to retain your Sugarverse account but delete specific types of data collected through the app, you can do so through the following methods:

  • Delete Profile Photos & Media: Open the app > Profile > Edit Profile (or Photo Album Manager) > Tap any photo slot > Select Delete Photo. The media is removed immediately from our servers.
  • Delete Chat & Conversation History: Open the chat conversation > Tap the options menu (three dots) > Select Unmatch / Delete Chat. Your message history with that user is permanently deleted.
  • Reset Roaming & Location Data: Open Travel Mode > Tap Reset to Current Location or disable location permissions in your Android device settings.
  • Email Request for Selective Data Purge: Send an email to privacy@sugarverse.app with the subject "Selective Data Deletion Request". State your registered email and specify the exact data you want removed (e.g., activity history, search preferences, or specific communication records). We will purge the requested data within 48 hours while keeping your core account active.

What Data Is Deleted Upon Full Account Closure

  • Profile Data: Name, age, gender, location, bio, seeking preferences, and relationship goals.
  • Photos & Media: All uploaded photos, identity verification selfie records, and verification tokens.
  • Social Interactions: Matches, swiping history, and sent/received chat messages.
  • Subscription Tokens: Active app session tokens and local device identifiers.

Data Retention Policy

Upon deletion, all profile data and media are permanently purged from our primary database and file storage immediately. Backup archives are purged within 30 days. No personal data is retained for commercial or marketing purposes.
