Sugarverse Child Safety Standards & CSAE Prevention Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2026

Sugarverse is strictly an 18+ adult matchmaking and dating platform. We maintain zero tolerance for any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), grooming, or underage access.

1. Strict Age Restriction (18+ Only)

Sugarverse is exclusively designed for legal consenting adults aged 18 and older. Minors (individuals under the age of 18) are strictly prohibited from creating accounts, accessing the application, or participating in any community features.

  • All users must declare and verify that they are at least 18 years old upon registration.
  • Accounts found or suspected to belong to minors are immediately terminated and permanently banned.

2. Zero Tolerance for CSAE & CSAM

Sugarverse strictly prohibits:

  • The creation, upload, transmission, possession, or distribution of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Any communication, behavior, or attempt aimed at the grooming, exploitation, or sexualization of minors.
  • Sharing or soliciting explicit images or communications involving individuals under the age of 18.

Any account involved in such activities will be immediately terminated with all access blocked permanently.

3. In-App Reporting & Safety Mechanisms

Sugarverse provides robust, accessible in-app safety controls allowing users to report any concerns immediately:

  • Profile Reporting: Every profile card and view includes an immediate Report button to flag inappropriate content or suspected underage accounts.
  • Chat Reporting: Users can report and block any message or conversation directly inside the chat interface.
  • Direct Safety Contact: Users can contact our dedicated safety team at support.sugarverse@gmail.com.

All reports involving potential child safety violations are treated with the highest priority and investigated within 24 hours.

4. Mandatory Law Enforcement & NCMEC Reporting

In full compliance with regional, national, and international child protection laws, Sugarverse cooperates with law enforcement authorities and child protection organizations worldwide:

  • Any identified instances of CSAM or child exploitation are immediately reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via CyberTipline, as well as relevant regional and national law enforcement agencies.
  • Data and digital evidence associated with reported violations are securely preserved to assist legal authorities in investigations and prosecutions.
